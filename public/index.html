APS AI uses artificial intelligence to make finding school information simple and accessible.

It brings together information from official school websites and documents to provide clear answers, help you discover important updates, and explore everything your school has to offer — all in one place.

1. Your School. Everything in One Place.

No more searching through multiple webpages, scattered documents or different sources to find what you need. APS AI brings important school information together in one convenient place, making it easier to discover school updates, explore academic information, find important contacts and get answers to your questions — all through one simple experience.

Your Privacy Matters.

Your trust matters to us. APS AI is designed to make accessing school information convenient while respecting your privacy. We believe finding answers should feel simple, comfortable and secure. Discover how APS AI handles your information and the measures in place to protect your privacy.

Clear Answers. Official School Sources.

School information should be easy to find and easy to understand. APS AI helps you discover answers using information from official school websites and documents made available to the platform. From school activities and academic details to facilities, administration and important announcements, find relevant information without having to search through multiple pages yourself.

No App to Download.

No installations. No complicated setup. No additional application to worry about. APS AI works directly in your web browser, whether you’re using a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop computer. Simply open the website, ask your question and explore the information you need — whenever and wherever you need it.
